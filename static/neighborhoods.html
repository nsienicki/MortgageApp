Typical home values are Zillow estimates for the middle third of homes in each area. Each trend line uses its own scale. Payments use this month's average 30-year rate; they're estimates, not quotes.

Sources

  • Home values: Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI), Zillow Group
  • Listings, sales and days on market: Redfin, a national real estate brokerage
  • Same-home prices: FHFA House Price Index, Federal Housing Finance Agency
  • Mortgage rates: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, via FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis
  • Household income: U.S. Census Bureau, via FRED

About

Homebuyer Market Lens is an independent project and isn't affiliated with Zillow, Redfin, the FHFA, Freddie Mac or the Census Bureau. Figures are estimates for general information. They aren't financial advice or a loan quote.
